Why Baddawg Grow

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Built from the ground up for growers who value privacy as much as yield.

100% Offline

Zero network permissions. Your grow data never leaves the device. No cloud sync, no accounts, no server to breach.

Sensor Dashboard

Pair BLE sensors or log manually. Real-time temp, RH, CO2, and light monitoring with VPD heatmap visualization.

AI Plant Diagnosis

Snap a photo, identify nutrient deficiencies and disease on-device. 97% accuracy. No upload required.

Compliance Tracker

25-state law database. Plant counts, possession limits, and PDF audit reports for legal peace of mind.

On-Device AI Chat

Ask grow questions to a local LLM. Gemma 2B runs entirely on your phone. No data ever leaves the device.

Harvest Countdown

Strain-based flowering estimates with milestone notifications. Trichome maturity AI tells you when to chop.

Community (Opt-In)

Pseudonymous profiles, grow journals, strain reviews, and Q&A. Your identity stays yours — always.

17,000+ Strain Library

Browse breeder data, terpene profiles, effects, and flavors. Live partner pricing from local dispensaries.
